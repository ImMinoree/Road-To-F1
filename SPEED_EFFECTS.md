# Cockpit speed perception

Normal Play in `/Game/RoadToF1/Maps/DesertKarting_Visual` applies cosmetic speed cues to the locally controlled player's cockpit. The four AI karts, driving physics, track geometry, race gate and audio are unchanged.

`ULocalKartDriverVisuals` reads `AProofPawn::GetDrivingSpeed()`. Effects begin above 350 cm/s (12.6 km/h) and smoothly approach full strength at the existing acceleration/drag equilibrium of 1343.75 cm/s (48.375 km/h). Reverse does not trigger them. The 1600 cm/s hard speed cap remains unchanged.

The reference cockpit widens smoothly from 110 to 115 degrees. The legacy cockpit widens from 90 to 96 degrees. Vibration is bounded to 0.025 degrees pitch, 0.018 degrees yaw and 0.035 degrees roll, with less than 0.04 cm combined translation. This is speed-scaled buffeting on the existing smooth road, not a new simulation of engine RPM or surface roughness. No accumulated camera drift, head bob or collision-root movement is introduced. A stop or race reset removes the effect immediately.

`/Game/RoadToF1/SpeedEffects/M_KartPeripheralSpeed` applies a very small radial blur after tonemapping. It keeps the centre clear, fades out within 2–4.5 m camera depth to protect the cockpit, and rejects near-geometry sample taps. The material uses three extra colour taps only where the effect is active. Conventional full-screen motion blur remains disabled. No speed lines, particles, new roadside objects or road texture changes were added.

The **Speed perception** properties on the existing LocalDriverVisuals component expose `bSpeedEffectsEnabled`, `SpeedFOVBoost`, `SpeedVibrationScale` and `PeripheralBlurScale`. Set either scale to zero to disable that individual effect. Disabling all speed effects restores the authored view on the next tick. Development launches can also use `-NoSpeedEffects`. Existing fixed-pose art capture flags bypass the speed effects for reproducible cockpit comparisons. Switching view targets or losing local ownership restores the view and clears blur. The always-cook label retains the material in cooked builds; a packaged build has not been validated.

Rebuild the material with Unreal's Python commandlet and `Tools/build_speed_material.py`. Run `Tools/validate_speed_pie.py` in a rendered editor with `-RacingAITest -RacingAIPlayerTest` for an actual driving check of pre-start, moving, disabled, re-enabled and reset states. This script uses the existing diagnostic player driver and never saves the map. Evidence is saved in `Evidence/SpeedEffects`.

Validation completed: the Development Editor build passed, as did the existing `RoadToF1.PlayerVisuals.OwnershipAndSteering` regression test (one success, zero warnings/failures). Actual PIE measured 110 degrees and zero blur before starting, 114.25 degrees with active effects while driving, 110 degrees and zero blur when disabled, active effects after re-enabling, and 110 degrees and zero blur after restarting the race. Camera restoration was within 0.001 cm. Five pawns remained present and map file hashes were unchanged. Four 1600×900 cockpit PNGs were decoded and visually inspected for the driving and reset views. `Tools/verify_speed_evidence.py` verifies the saved reports and image files. Earlier test-harness failures are retained in the attempt logs; `pie_verified.log` and `pie_validation.json` describe the successful run.
